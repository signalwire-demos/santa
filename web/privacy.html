Privacy — Santa's Workshop

This is a technology demonstration of an AI voice agent. It is run by SignalWire. It is not a real letter to Santa, and nothing said here reaches the North Pole.

This experience is intended for visitors aged 13 and over, or for children under 13 who have a parent or guardian with them. A grown-up should be present for any child using it.

What the demo collects while you talk to Santa

  • Your voice, which is not recorded. The audio is carried over the SignalWire network so the AI agent can hear and answer you, and it is not saved. No recording of the call is kept.
  • A written summary of the conversation -- the gifts discussed and the one chosen -- plus an automatic check that the service was not misused. It contains no recording and no estimate of your age.
  • Whatever you say out loud — including a first name if you give one, and the gift you choose. The agent uses the name to reply and to look things up during the call.
  • Technical connection data needed to place the call, such as your IP address and browser type.

What it does not collect

  • No camera, ever. The demo asks for your microphone only. Your browser should never prompt you for camera access; if it does, something is wrong — please close the page and tell us.
  • No account, no sign-up, no email address, no postal address.
  • No advertising, no tracking across other websites, no sale of data.

Children under 13

We ask every visitor to confirm their age before the microphone is turned on. If you are under 13, a parent or guardian must be present and must agree before you start. The call itself is not recorded; only a short written summary of the gift conversation is kept. A parent or guardian who wants anything from their child's session reviewed or deleted can contact us at the address below and we will do it.

Who to contact

Questions, or a request to delete a session: privacy@signalwire.com.
